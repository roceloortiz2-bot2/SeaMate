About SeaMate

SeaMate is an independent helper app. Calculations are estimates and depend on your employment contract, company policy, flag-state requirements and applicable rules. It is not an official payroll or MLC compliance system.

Daily Rest Hours

Enter total rest periods taken within one 24-hour period.

TOTAL REST ENTERED
10.0 hours
Daily total meets the 10-hour minimum.

Basic MLC 2006 standard: at least 10 hours of rest in any 24-hour period and 77 hours in any 7-day period. Rest may generally be divided into no more than two periods, one of which must be at least 6 hours; the interval between consecutive rest periods must not exceed 14 hours. This simple daily-total tool does not verify rolling windows, timestamps, interruptions, exceptions or official records. It is not a compliance certificate.
